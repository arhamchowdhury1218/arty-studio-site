OIL PAINTING WORKSHOP

Learn the basics of oil painting through color mixing, matching, and brushwork.

    • Canvas (Size 40×50)

    • Drinks & Snacks

    • Oil Colors

    • Painting Mediums
      (additives that change paint properties)

    • Brushes & Palettes

    • Material Use

    • Color Blending

    • Reference-Based Matching
      (how to match colors off an image or a reference)

  • For all ages - Adults & Children

  • Up to 4-5 Days | 2 Hours Per Session

    Paintings can be left at our Studio between & after sessions for safe-keeping. (Maximum 2 weeks)
